How do you want to use Setu?

Over the internet (Hotel Setu Cloud)

For hotels without a computer: billing from the phone. Needs internet (Wi-Fi or mobile data).
New hotel: Open Cloud → Sign Up → hotel name, mobile, password and the vendor's Master Key.
Already created: sign in with Hotel Code + mobile + password.

On the restaurant Wi-Fi (hotel with a counter PC)

For waiters, cashiers and the kitchen. The counter PC must be on and the phone on the same Wi-Fi. The counter PC address is shown in Setu → Settings (G) → "Phone / Tablet Connect" (e.g. 192.168.1.6).

This choice is remembered. To change it, keep pressing Back in Setu and this screen comes back.
